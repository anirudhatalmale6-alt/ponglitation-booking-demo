PongLiTation · Connect Domain

Point ponglitation.com at your site 🌐

This links the domain you bought to your live site. It's two short parts — tell Render your domain, then paste two records into Namecheap. I've written the exact values. About 10 minutes, then the internet does the rest.

1 Tell Render your domain

≈ 2 minutes
  1. In Render, open your ponglitation service.
  2. Left menu → Settings → scroll down to Custom Domains.
  3. Click Add Custom Domain, type ponglitation.com, save.
  4. Click Add Custom Domain again, type www.ponglitation.com, save.
  5. Render will now show the DNS records it wants (an A record for the root and a CNAME for www). Keep this tab open — you'll copy from it in Part 2.
These are the values Render normally gives — yours should match. If Render shows something slightly different, always go with what Render shows.
TypeHostPoints to
A@216.24.57.1
CNAMEwwwponglitation.onrender.com

2 Add the records at Namecheap

≈ 5 minutes
  1. Go to Namecheap → Domain List and click Manage next to ponglitation.com.
  2. Click the Advanced DNS tab.
  3. Under Host Records, delete the records Namecheap added by default (usually a CNAME “www → parkingpage.namecheap.com” and a “URL Redirect / @”). Click the trash-can icon on each. This is important — they'll conflict otherwise.
  4. Click Add New Record and add these two (use Render's values if different):
    TypeHostValueTTL
    A Record@216.24.57.1Automatic
    CNAME Recordwwwponglitation.onrender.comAutomatic
  5. Click the green Save All Changes (the little checkmark).
Tip: on the CNAME value, Namecheap sometimes adds a dot at the end automatically — that's fine, leave it.

That's it — the internet takes over ⏳

Back in Render, the domain will move from “Verifying” to a green check, and it sets up the secure padlock (SSL) automatically. This usually takes a few minutes but can take up to an hour while the change spreads across the internet — totally normal.
