Your booking engine — working

Here's the real system in action (not a mockup). Scroll through to see it.

Step 1

A real booking, confirmed

A client picks a service, date and time, and books. They get an instant confirmation with a unique reference and the time in Central Time. The slot is now reserved — no one else can take it.

Step 2

The automatic confirmation email

This lands in the client's inbox the second they book — branded, with their booking details. A reminder email also goes out the day before the session. Zero manual follow-up from you.

Step 3

Your no-code Owner Dashboard

See every booking (name, email, phone, notes), and cancel any of them. No code, no spreadsheets.

Step 4

Change your hours & prices anytime

Edit each day's times, block off holidays, and update prices yourself — the live site updates instantly.
